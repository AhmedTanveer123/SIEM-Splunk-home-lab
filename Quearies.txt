Quearies

1\. Windows Security log collection

index=* host="ANONYMOUS" sourcetype="WinEventLog:Security"
| stats count by EventCode
| sort - count


2\. SMB activity from Parrot OS


index=* host="ANONYMOUS" sourcetype="WinEventLog:Security" EventCode=5145
| search "192.168.205.133"
| stats count


3\. Identify source IP, account, and share


index=* host="ANONYMOUS" sourcetype="WinEventLog:Security" EventCode=5145
| search "192.168.205.133"
| table _time Account_Name IpAddress ShareName
| sort - _time


4\. Investigate test file activity


index=* host="ANONYMOUS" sourcetype="WinEventLog:Security" EventCode=5145
| search "192.168.205.133"
| search "soc-test.txt" OR "suspicious-test.ps1"
| table _time _raw
| sort - _time


5\. Inspect raw SMB events


index=* host="ANONYMOUS" sourcetype="WinEventLog:Security" EventCode=5145
| search "192.168.205.133"
| table _time _raw
| sort - _time
| head 10


6\. Count events associated with the test files


index=* host="ANONYMOUS" sourcetype="WinEventLog:Security" EventCode=5145
| search "192.168.205.133"
| search "soc-test.txt" OR "suspicious-test.ps1"
| stats count



Important: These are the queries that returned results during your investigation. The count may change depending on the selected time range and newly collected events.
